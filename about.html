ABOUT THIS SITE

Everyday math, made clearer.

ClearDay Tools is a small collection of free calculators for practical questions: working out percentages and discounts, converting common length measurements, and counting words and characters.

Our approach

We aim to keep each tool focused, explain what its result means, and make it usable on phones as well as computers. The calculations run in your browser; the text you enter into the word counter is not sent to a server by this site.

Accuracy and limitations

We test our tools against basic worked examples, but a calculator should not replace professional advice or a check of the terms that apply to your situation. Results may be rounded for display. Always verify important calculations independently.

Editorial standards

Our guides are written to explain the underlying arithmetic in plain language. We will correct unclear or inaccurate explanations when identified. For questions or corrections, see Contact.
