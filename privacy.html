YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy information

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This page describes how the current ClearDay Tools website handles information. It must be reviewed and updated if the site adds analytics, advertising, forms, or other services.

Information you enter into the tools

The calculators on this site run in your browser. Values entered into the percentage, discount, and length calculators, and text entered in the word counter, are used to show results on your device. This site does not send those values to its own server.

Cookies and analytics

This site loads the Google AdSense script with the publisher ID configured by the site owner. No ad units are currently configured, so this script alone does not display ads. Google may process information from your browser or use cookies and similar technologies when its script loads. Review Google's privacy information for details. The web hosting provider may also process technical connection information, such as IP address and request details, to deliver and secure the site.

Advertising services

AdSense is integrated for site verification, but the site currently has no ad units. Before serving ads, the site owner should update this notice to describe the advertising setup and available privacy choices. Consent requirements may apply depending on where visitors are located. Visitors should review Google's privacy disclosures.

External resources

The site currently requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google's servers to load those fonts. You can disable remote fonts in your browser settings; system fonts will be used as a fallback.

Changes and contact

This notice may be revised when the website changes. For privacy questions, contact us at sabariari639@gmail.com.
